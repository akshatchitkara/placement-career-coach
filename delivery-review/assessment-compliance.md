# Assessment compliance matrix

**Source of truth:** `PM Assignment1.docx` (the original assessment, found at `Downloads/PM Assignment1.docx`, last modified 9 October). The blueprint (`docs/00_PROJECT_BLUEPRINT.md`) was used as the implementation reference, and its own traceability matrix (Section 21) lists 15 requirements, all of which are covered below. Requirement IDs are mine: A for the scenario and product description, B for Task 1, C for Task 2, D for submission.

## Method and score

- 30 requirements, **equal weights** (the assessment gives none). This is an internal estimate, **not an official assessment score**.
- Only PASS counts as compliant. PARTIAL, FAIL and NOT VERIFIED each count as zero.
- **Compliance now: 26 of 30 = 86.7%.** 2 partial, 0 failing, 2 not verified.
- Reconstructed starting point at the start of this review: 13 of 30 = 43.3% (11 failing, 4 partial). The product note was empty and Industry-ready was unreachable.
- **Mandatory work only** (Task 1 and Task 2, excluding the assessor's own judgement B10): 22 of 22 verified PASS.
- Status definitions: PASS means executed or inspected evidence shows the requirement is met; PARTIAL means part is met; FAIL means it is not met; NOT VERIFIED means it cannot be established from the repository or by me.

## Notes

- The brief's scenario says a student 'enters a chosen career field' and gives non-CS examples. The product narrows this on purpose to four computer science careers (blueprint 1.3, note section 1). An assessor who reads the examples literally could rate A1 lower than PARTIAL.
- Task 1 was written by the assistant during this review. Its figures were checked against the code, but the author should read it and confirm it says what they mean.
- The questions behind every level are drafts that no person has reviewed. That is not an assessment requirement, but a level is only as trustworthy as its questions.
